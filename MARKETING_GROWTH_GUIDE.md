## 📊 GROWTH METRICS & KPIs

### Acquisition Metrics:
```
- Website visitors
- Signup rate (target: 10%)
- Activation rate (target: 70%)
- Trial-to-paid (target: 25%)
- CAC (target: <$50)
- Payback period (target: <3 meses)
```

### Engagement Metrics:
```
- Daily Active Users (DAU)
- Weekly Active Users (WAU)
- MAU/DAU ratio (target: >0.3)
- Session length (target: >10 min)
- Features used per session (target: >3)
- NPS score (target: >50)
```

### Revenue Metrics:
```
- MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)
- ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue)
- ARPU (Average Revenue Per User)
- LTV (Lifetime Value, target: >$500)
- Churn rate (target: <5%/mês)
- MRR Growth Rate (target: >20%/mês)
```

### Funnel:
```
100,000 visitors/mês
    ↓ (10% signup)
10,000 signups
    ↓ (70% activation)
7,000 activated
    ↓ (25% paid)
1,750 paying users
    × $50 ARPU
= $87,500 MRR
```
